about

I’m Stephen Awuah, a software and protocol engineer from Ghana 🇬🇭. I build products end to end — from the customer problem and product surface to the payment, data, and distributed-system foundations underneath. Right now that means real-estate infrastructure with FlexDown, and the universal payment rail for AI agents with ChainPay, where Solana is the first settlement layer.

what i bring

  • Product-minded systems engineering: moving from an ambiguous problem to a dependable technical shape.
  • Protocol and payment work: policy boundaries, wallet signing flows, token-program compatibility, receipts, and developer-facing APIs.
  • Community building: practical paths for Ghanaian builders to learn, ship, and contribute through Solana Ghana.
  • Clear technical communication: I write the architecture down, explain trade-offs, and make the next decision easier for a team.
