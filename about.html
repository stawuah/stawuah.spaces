about

I’m Stephen Awuah, a developing protocol engineer from Ghana 🇬🇭, working where Solana, programmable payments, tokenisation and distributed systems meet. I founded FlexDown, built ChainPay’s policy-controlled Devnet and MCP payment flows, contributed to Chainlink open source, and wrote Token Economics from First Principles. I am also building Solana Ghana Community, where a campus introduction drew more than 40 members in a day and helped one of them into Solana Fall School.

what i bring

  • Product-minded systems engineering: I move from an ambiguous problem to a dependable technical shape.
  • Protocol and payment work: I build policy boundaries, wallet signing flows, token-program compatibility, receipts, and developer-facing APIs.
  • Community building: I create practical paths for Ghanaian builders to learn, ship, and contribute through Solana Ghana.
  • Clear technical communication: I write the architecture down, explain trade-offs, and make the next decision easier for a team.
